Version 1.0.0 (build 11) is the first public release.

MiniGolf: Putt & Bloom is an offline, single-player mini golf game. No account, sign-in, advertising, in-app purchase or subscription is required.

Languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Turkish, Arabic, Portuguese, French and Spanish. The initial language follows the supported system preference, falling back to English. Use the globe icon in the same top row as the club brand on the start screen or in the course options to switch without losing the current round.

Review route:
1. Choose Classic mode. Drag the pull control to set direction and power together; release to putt. No extra confirmation is required. Classic mode shows the putter beside the ball while pulling and a short follow-through on release; this is presentation only. Classic mode arcs over obstacles without rebounds. Out-of-bounds shots return to the last safe point.
2. Wait for the ball to stop before the next shot. After holing out, tap to continue. A hole-in-one replay can be skipped.
3. The map button opens all three practice courses. The leaf button, Play & collect, opens six bank-shot challenges, My garden and the quote collection. All six challenges are immediately selectable and retain their bounce rules. Cozy mode also retains the existing bounce gameplay.
4. Completing a hole grants one seed and 1–3 points from the earned stars. Seeds plant flowers in the nine-plot garden. Points and seeds cannot be bought, transferred or redeemed for money. There is no points marketplace in this release.
5. Sound effects and Haptics are independent settings, enabled by default. Putting plays a soft sound and a light impact haptic. A retained, view-associated generator prepares at the start of each pull and fires once on a valid shot. Haptics use their own switch; Reduce Motion disables animated follow-through and other motion, while leaving the independent haptics preference active.
6. Privacy policy opens an offline policy and a clickable public HTTPS policy link from the hand icon in the same top row as the club brand on the start screen and from course options. It pauses the same round while open.

Storage: Points and collections are saved in this device’s system Keychain with a local recovery copy. Existing local collections migrate automatically. A receipt prevents awarding the same restored win twice. The records do not sync through iCloud or migrate to another device from backup; no permanent-storage or server-recovery promise is made. The app does not actively upload game data.

iPhone Duo is the main experience highlighted in this release. On Duo, open the inner display for a roomier course, close to continue the same round on the outer display, and use Handheld mode for separated course and control areas. The app preserves ball position and stroke count across display and window changes. Important controls avoid active reserved regions; an inactive decorative seam no longer covers handheld-mode results or controls. The compact stroke card uses a clear numeric count with a complete accessibility label. Layout adapts to available window space and reserved regions. Handheld mode is a manual separated-control equivalent, available from course options. All courses are playable on standard iPhone and iPad; no folding pose is required to unlock content.
