How do I play?

Drag the pull control with one finger to set direction and power together. Release to putt. Wait for the ball to stop before taking another shot. When the win result appears, tap to continue.

Which devices and languages are supported?

FoldPutt supports iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, including window and reserved-region adaptation for iPhone Duo. All courses can be completed on standard devices without a particular folding posture. The current in-app interface is in Simplified Chinese. Store descriptions and this help page in English do not mean the app interface has been translated.

Where are courses and collections?

Use the map button on the course to choose a practice course. Tap the leaf button labeled “玩法与收藏” to open six bank-shot puzzles, the garden, and the quote collection.

What are seeds and the garden?

Completing a course earns a seed for the nine-plot garden. Seeds cannot be purchased, redeemed for money, or transferred. Mistakes do not consume lives and there is no paid revival.

How do I turn off sound, haptics, or motion?

Sound and haptics have separate switches in the course options. The game respects the system Reduce Motion setting.

How do I delete data or recover progress?

Progress and collections are stored locally. Deleting the app removes local data; offloading it while retaining documents does not clear the save. System backups and migration follow your Apple settings. The developer does not provide a cloud recovery service for game progress.

How do I report a problem?

Email waynelessdo@gmail.com with your app version, device model, system version, steps to reproduce the problem, and any necessary screenshot. For layout issues, describe the active display, window size, and text-size setting. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or unrelated private information.
