MiniGolf: Putt & Bloom Privacy Policy
Updated: October 24, 2026
Provider: Wayne
Support and privacy: waynelessdo@gmail.com

1. Offline play, no account
MiniGolf is a single-player mini golf game. No registration or login is required. Core gameplay works offline. There are no ads, in-app purchases, third-party analytics or advertising tracking. The app does not actively send game data to the developer.

2. Data stored on your device
The app saves ball position, strokes and round progress locally. It also stores scores, points, seeds, garden, quotes and preferences for appearance, sound and haptics. Collection progress and points are stored in the system Keychain, with a local recovery copy. This data supports gameplay and is not used to identify you, build advertising profiles or track you across apps.

3. System information and permissions
The app uses window size, safe areas and display reserved regions to adapt the interface, and local timing for gameplay. It does not actively send this information to the developer or request access to contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

4. Retention, deletion and backups
Game data stays on the device. Keychain records may remain after deleting and reinstalling the app. Deleting the app does not guarantee deletion of points or collection records. These Keychain records do not sync through iCloud or migrate to another device from a backup. Device erasure, system changes or restoring a different device may lose this data. Apple may process backups and migration according to your settings. The developer provides no account-based cloud sync or server recovery and cannot promise permanent storage.

5. Support
If you contact waynelessdo@gmail.com, your email, issue description and attachments are used only to handle and answer your request. Do not send passwords, codes or unrelated sensitive information. Support messages are kept as needed to handle the request. You may request deletion through the same address, subject to applicable law.

6. External services
Visiting the support website or sending email may cause your browser, hosting provider, mail provider or Apple to process access, communications, backups or diagnostics under their own policies. This is separate from the game uploading progress.

7. Children and updates
No age or registration details are required, and the app does not aim to collect children's personal information. Contact the address above with any concern. The policy and date will be updated when functionality or data practices change.

This policy is available offline from the start screen and course options.
